Targeting

This page translates portfolio review into action by showing outreach readiness, recommended ask levels, and next-best actions for coded records.

Audience: Gift Officers + Managers
Executive summary The strongest near-term opportunities sit in upgrade and renewal lanes. Recommended ask totals are planning figures, not committed revenue.

What this page is for

  • Prioritize outreach in the next 30–90 days
  • Separate upgrade, renewal, recovery, and stewardship lanes
  • Estimate planning volume by recommended ask amount
  • Translate analysis into next-step action

How to interpret totals

  • Top targets = records with highest readiness
  • Recommended ask total = planning sum only
  • Upgrade / renewal counts = recommended action lanes
  • These totals do not equal booked gifts or closed revenue

Key takeaways

Finding What it means Recommended action
Upgrade opportunities remain strong Several records have both readiness and capacity Prioritize visit/proposal planning for top upgrade records
Renewal work still matters operationally Smaller asks can sustain continuity and reduce lapse risk Sequence renewal outreach before broader escalation
Recovery records need different handling Not every record should receive a larger ask now Route recovery cases to stewardship-first workflow

Decision guide

  • If A-priority records increase, expand near-term outreach capacity
  • If renewal counts increase, protect continuity before upgrading asks
  • If recovery volume rises, slow escalation and increase stewardship

Method and limitations

  • Targeting scores are synthetic and modeled
  • Ask totals are planning totals only
  • One household may contribute more than one coded record
  • Target type is a recommendation, not a completed action
