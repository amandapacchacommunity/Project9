How to Read Totals on This Page

Relationship totals differ from financial totals. They describe record structure and connected entities.

Type: Individual

Timeframe Relevance: 30–90 day stewardship, longer institutional coordination cycle.

Relationship Scale Meaning

Scale Definition Use
Individual Single coded donor record Personal giving history and stewardship tracking
Household Grouped family/shared context Coordinated cultivation and ask timing
Foundation Linked institutional entity Proposal workflow and separate institutional tracking
Status Current, lapsed, linked, partner Relationship interpretation rather than financial measurement

Timeframe Definitions

Measure Typical Window Why It Matters
Last meaningful touch 30 / 60 / 90+ days Helps assess recency of relationship management
Household review Quarterly Reduces duplicated outreach and conflicting asks
Foundation planning 90–180+ days Reflects longer institutional cycles
Relationship health review 12–24 months Useful for portfolio continuity and coverage analysis

Totals Clarification Notes

  • These totals are structural, not financial.
  • One donor can be linked to one household and one foundation.
  • Entity examples are coded records, not confidential names.
  • Relationship counts do not equal giving counts.
  • Foundation-linked counts may overlap with donor-linked counts.

Interpretation Notes

A relationship page is meant to show who is connected to whom, not how much revenue is closed. A linked foundation or household should be understood as relationship structure. These records can inform strategy and coordination, but they should not be read as standalone financial totals.
