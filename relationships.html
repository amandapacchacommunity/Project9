Relationship Scale Meaning

Scale Definition Use
Individual Single coded donor record Best for personal giving history and one-to-one stewardship
Household Grouped family or shared giving context Useful for coordinated cultivation and ask timing
Foundation Institutional or family foundation-linked entity Requires proposal-style planning and separate tracking
Relationship Status Current, lapsed, linked, or partner Helps interpret readiness and stewardship priority
