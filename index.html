Risk Scale and Assessment Window

Scale Meaning Assessment Basis
Low: 0–30 Stable fulfillment profile with limited immediate concern Strong fulfillment history, current or near-current pledge aging, active membership, recent touch within 90 days
Medium: 31–60 Watchlist candidate needing closer stewardship cadence Some delay, mixed retention, moderate aging, or declining engagement across the last 6–12 months
High: 61–100 Elevated likelihood of missed fulfillment or lapse-related attrition Older unpaid balances, lapsed membership, overdue pledge aging, low recent engagement, weak 12-month fulfillment profile

Primary assessment window: prior 12 months for giving, fulfillment, and engagement trend; current pledge cycle for aging and outstanding balance; last 90 days for touchpoint recency; and next 30 days for action scheduling.

Methodology Notes

This synthetic dashboard uses coded donor records and a rules-based advancement risk framework rather than a lending-style underwriting model. “Credit risk” here means the likelihood that a donor may delay, reduce, or fail to complete a pledge, or may lapse from an ongoing giving relationship. Scores combine fulfillment, stewardship, engagement, and timing indicators to support advancement decision-making.
