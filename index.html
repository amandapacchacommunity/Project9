Risk Overview

This page supports portfolio review by showing where pledge and retention risk are concentrated, what the totals mean, and which records need attention first.

Audience: Advancement + Leadership
Executive summary High-risk exposure is concentrated in a small number of records, especially in Major Gifts. The main decision supported here is where to focus stewardship and follow-up effort first.

What this page is for

Use this page for monthly or quarterly portfolio review.

  • Identify where open-balance risk is concentrated
  • See how many records fall into each risk band
  • Prioritize follow-up on the highest-risk records
  • Separate exposure totals from revenue totals

How to interpret totals

  • Portfolio records = coded records, not deduped households
  • Outstanding exposure = open pledge balance, not booked revenue
  • Average retention = average percentage, not a summed total
  • High-risk records = count of records in the high-risk band

Key takeaways

Finding What it means Recommended action
High-risk records are limited in count but material in exposure Risk is concentrated rather than evenly spread Review top overdue records first
Lapsed records tend to carry weaker retention Renewal and stewardship should precede larger asks Route these to renewal or recovery workflow
Low-risk records remain important opportunity areas Not all portfolio work is defensive; some records are ready for upgrade Coordinate with targeting page for next-step asks

Decision guide

  • If high-risk exposure rises, review overdue major gift records
  • If lapsed counts rise, prioritize renewal work
  • If low-risk records stay strong, review upgrade potential

Method and limitations

  • Synthetic data for demonstration
  • Record-level counts are not deduped to household level
  • Exposure totals are not revenue totals
  • Risk scores are modeled, not production-scored
