Risk Scale Meaning

Tier Score Meaning
Low 0–30 Healthy fulfillment pattern; typically suitable for standard or upgrade asks.
Medium 31–60 Some concern; may need pacing, reminders, or stewardship support.
High 61–100 Elevated risk of delay, lapse, or non-fulfillment; stewardship-first review recommended.

Totals Clarification Notes

  • Exposure totals are not gift revenue totals.
  • Visible counts change with filters.
  • Retention values are averages, not summed percentages.
  • Record counts are not deduped to household or entity level.
  • Timeframe changes can alter both scores and averages.

Interpretation Notes

This page is designed for risk review rather than forecasting revenue. A high exposure total means a larger open-balance concentration is visible in the current selection. It does not mean those balances are lost, nor does it equal campaign revenue. Likewise, a high record count does not necessarily mean many unique donor relationships, because one household or foundation relationship may be represented through multiple linked coded records.
