How Risk Was Assessed

Synthetic scoring model used for portfolio demonstration.

Indicator Assessment Rule Weight
Past unpaid pledges Any prior unpaid commitment in the review window 30
Late payment history Average payment delay above 30 days 20
Lapsed membership Membership marked as lapsed or expired 15
Declining giving trend Most recent giving lower than earlier periods 15
Low engagement No recent stewardship touch or participation 10
Stretch ask size Open pledge materially above prior giving pattern 10

Risk Scale Meaning

Tier Score Range Meaning
Low 0–30 Strong fulfillment pattern, limited signs of lapse or delay, suitable for upgrade or standard asks.
Medium 31–60 Some payment or retention concerns, usually workable with reminders, pacing, or smaller ask structure.
High 61–100 Elevated risk of delayed payment, non-fulfillment, or lapse; requires stewardship-first review.

Timeframe Definitions

Metric Timeframe Why It Matters
Risk review window 12 / 24 / 36 months Longer windows capture more pledge behavior but may dilute recent changes.
Membership status Current cycle / last 12 months Shows whether renewal is current or recent lapse recovery is needed.
Retention rate Trailing selected review window Measures giving continuity within the chosen period.
Past due status Current open pledge aging Helps distinguish mild lateness from serious collections risk.
Giving trend Most recent period vs prior periods Identifies whether donor momentum is strengthening or weakening.

Interpretation Notes

This synthetic dashboard uses coded records and weighted scoring to simulate advancement portfolio review. In practice, an advancement manager could align the review window to campaign cycles, annual membership terms, or pledge schedules. A 12-month view is best for near-term targeting, a 24-month view is helpful for trend review, and a 36-month view is useful for long-cycle major gift assessment.
